FAQ

Questions you might have.

What does HAIVEXA do?

HAIVEXA provides practical technology, AI and digital solutions for businesses. This includes websites, customer experience tools, automation, data solutions and custom digital tools.

Who founded HAIVEXA?

HAIVEXA was founded by Christal Haines. The business is built around a practical, problem-first approach to technology and digital solutions.

Do I need to know exactly what solution I need?

No. You can start by explaining the problem, idea or process you want to improve. HAIVEXA will ask the right questions and help identify a practical solution.

Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. HAIVEXA works with small businesses, solo business owners and service providers that need practical digital solutions.

Can HAIVEXA improve an existing website?

Yes. Website fixes, redesigns, mobile improvements, forms, buttons and deployment issues can all be assessed.

Can you help with AI and automation?

Yes. AI and automation can be explored where they provide a useful solution to a business problem, such as repetitive tasks, workflows or customer experiences.

Can you connect my website to WhatsApp?

Yes. HAIVEXA can add enquiry buttons and structured, pre-filled WhatsApp messages so customers can contact your business more easily.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope. A small website update is much quicker than a multi-page website or custom digital tool. A realistic timeline will be provided before work begins.

What payment structure do you use?

For standard projects, the starting payment structure is typically a 50% deposit before work begins and the remaining 50% before final launch or handover. Small jobs may require full payment upfront. The final arrangement will be included in your quotation.

How many revisions are included?

Standard website projects include up to two rounds of reasonable revisions based on the agreed scope. Additional revisions or changes outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately.

Do I need to understand technical terms?

No. Explain your business problem or idea in normal language. HAIVEXA will handle the technical side and recommend a practical approach.
